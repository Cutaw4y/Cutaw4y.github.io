Cutaway & Gölgedefter

Cutaway is an English educational YouTube channel that explains the science and engineering behind everyday things and giant systems, step by step. youtube.com/@thecutaw4y

Gölgedefter is a Turkish documentary channel about lost civilizations, history and unexplained events, told with their sources. youtube.com/@golgedefter

This site also hosts the information page for OtoYT, the private publishing tool the channels' owner uses to upload the channels' own videos, captions and thumbnails, manage their playlists, and read the channels' own performance statistics. It has no other users.
